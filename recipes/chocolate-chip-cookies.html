Chocolate Chip Cookies Recipes

Description

Soft, chewy cookies with crispy edges and plenty of chocolate in every bite.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup softened butter
  • 3/4 white sugar
  • 3/4 brown sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 2 1/4 cups all-purpose-flour
  • 1 teaspoon of baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon of salt
  • 2 cups chocolate chips

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 375F.
  2. Mix the butter, white sugar, and brown sugar until creamy.
  3. Beat in the eggs and vanilla.
  4. In a separate bow, stir together the flour baking soda, and salt.
  5. Gradually mix the dry ingredients into the butter mixture.
  6. Stir in the chocolate Chips.
  7. Drop rounded spoonfuls, until the edges are golden.
  8. Let the cookies cool for a while, than enjoy!
