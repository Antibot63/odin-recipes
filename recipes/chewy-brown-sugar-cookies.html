Chewy Brown Sugar Cookies Recipes

Description

Chewy and warm, with a crackly brown sugar crust, these sugar cookies take less than 30 minutes, start to finish.

Ingredients

  • 14 tablespoon butter, melted
  • 1 3/4 cups packed light brown sugar
  • 3/8 teaspoon table salt
  • 1 large egg
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 2 cups of flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda

Instructions

  1. Set oven to 350F
  2. In a large bowl, mix butter, brown sugar, and salt.
  3. Stir in eggs and vanilla until well combined.
  4. Add flour baking powder, and baking powder and stir until combined.
  5. Bake for 9-11 min, coli for 5 min.
  6. Enjoy!
