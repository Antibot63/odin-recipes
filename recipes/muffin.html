Muffin Recipes

Description

Basic Muffin with no toppings, soft and sweet. Can be made within 10 min + 15-20 min of bake time.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups of flour
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 3/4 cups milk
  • 1/2 cups oil
  • 2 large eggs

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350F. Line a muffin pan with paper liners; set aside
  2. In a small bowl, whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salted together
  3. In a medium bowl, whisk the milk, oil, eggs together until well combined
  4. Mix flour mixture to the wet mixture and stir with a spatula until combined
  5. Divide the batter evenly to each of the muffin cups.
  6. Bake for 20-25 min, use toothpick to check if insides are moist
  7. Take muffin out, and wait to coli
  8. Enjoy!
